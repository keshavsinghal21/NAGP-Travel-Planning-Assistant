# Things to Do in Singapore

## Unique Experiences

Singapore has different experiences for visitors who want to do
something beyond the usual sightseeing.

Some examples include:

- Local experiences
- Guided tours
- Food experiences
- Cultural activities
- Wellness activities
- Unique activities around the city

## City in Nature

Singapore combines city attractions with green spaces and nature.

Places and activities include:

- Gardens by the Bay
- Singapore Botanic Gardens
- National Orchid Garden
- Parks
- Nature reserves
- Outdoor activities

## Culture and Heritage

Singapore has different cultural neighbourhoods and heritage
areas to explore.

Some places include:

- Chinatown
- Little India
- Kampong Gelam
- Katong-Joo Chiat
- Civic District

Visitors can explore temples, mosques, churches, museums,
heritage buildings, local shops and traditional food in these
areas.

## Iconic Architecture

Singapore has many modern and well-known buildings and
landmarks.

Places to explore include:

- Marina Bay Sands
- Gardens by the Bay
- Marina Bay
- The Merlion
- Civic District

These areas are also suitable for sightseeing and photography.

Singapore has attractions and activities suitable for families

Some options include:

- Family-friendly attractions

Some places include:

- National Museum of Singapore
- National Gallery Singapore
- Peranakan Museum
- Art galleries
- Other museums around the Civic District

Popular areas include:

- Orchard Road

- Local shopping districts

Shopping options range from large shopping malls to local

## Neighbourhoods

### Chinatown

Chinatown is a heritage neighbourhood with temples, traditional
shops, murals, restaurants and food options.

Places to visit include:

- Buddha Tooth Relic Temple
- Sri Mariamman Temple
- Chinatown Complex Food Centre

### Civic District

The Civic District has museums, historic buildings and
architecture connected with Singapore's history.

Places include:

- National Gallery Singapore
- National Museum of Singapore
- The Arts House
- Other historic buildings

### Kampong Gelam

Kampong Gelam is a neighbourhood connected with Malay heritage,
culture, shops, cafes and street art.

The area is also known for its colourful streets and heritage
buildings.

### Katong-Joo Chiat

Katong-Joo Chiat is known for Peranakan culture, heritage
shophouses, food and cafes.

It is a good area for exploring local culture and food.

### Little India

Little India is known for Indian culture, food, shops,
religious sites and colourful streets.

Visitors can explore the neighbourhood on foot.

### Mandai

Mandai is an area associated with wildlife and nature
attractions.

Places include:

- Singapore Zoo
- Bird Paradise
- Other wildlife attractions

### Marina Bay

Marina Bay is one of Singapore's main sightseeing areas.

Things to see include:

- Marina Bay Sands
- Gardens by the Bay
- The Merlion
- Marina Bay waterfront

The area can be visited during the day and evening.

### Orchard Road

Orchard Road is one of Singapore's main shopping areas.

It has:

- Shopping malls
- International brands
- Local shops
- Restaurants
- Food options

### Sentosa

Sentosa is an island resort with beaches, entertainment and
family activities.

Some attractions include:

- Universal Studios Singapore
- Singapore Oceanarium
- Beaches
- Adventure activities

### Singapore River

The Singapore River is a historic area with walking areas,
restaurants and river experiences.

Visitors can explore the river area and nearby attractions.
